Who We Are

Roofing and Restoration, Owner-Led

Pine Ridge Roofing and Mitigation is a locally owned company based in Jacksonville, Texas, serving Cherokee County and the surrounding East Texas towns. Josh Jones owns it and runs it, and that is on purpose. When you hire Pine Ridge, you are hiring the person whose name is on the sign.

The work comes out of years of hands-on experience in the building and repair trades. Roofing and water restoration are focused, skilled work, so we built a company around doing them well rather than doing a little of everything.

We kept the scope simple for a reason. Roofing for homes and businesses, and water damage restoration from the first moisture reading through fresh paint. That is what we do, and we do all of it ourselves.
