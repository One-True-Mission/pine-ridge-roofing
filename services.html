Storm Damage

After the Storm, We Document It Right

When a storm hits your roof, the first job is figuring out what it actually did. We go over the roof, photograph the damage, and put together a detailed written scope. If you file a claim, that documentation is what your carrier needs, and we can meet your adjuster on site so everyone is looking at the same roof.

We work with all insurance carriers. What we do not do is step into the middle of your claim, because in Texas a roofer cannot act as your insurance adjuster on a roof it is also hired to repair. We handle the roof and the paperwork that documents it. The claim itself stays between you and your carrier, which is exactly where it should be.

Water Mitigation & Restoration

We Do the Whole Cycle, Not Half of It

Here is what usually happens after water damage. A mitigation company comes out, sets up fans, dries the structure, and leaves. Now you have a dry room with the drywall cut out, no insulation, and studs showing. To finish it, you go find a second contractor, get a second bid, and wait on a second schedule.

Pine Ridge does both halves. We dry the structure and we rebuild it, right through the final coat of paint. That means one company accountable for the whole job, one number to call, and a room that is actually finished when we are done, not just dry.

Our Process

Five Steps, One Company

This is exactly how we work a water damage job, in order, from the first reading to the last coat of paint.

1

Moisture Detection & Readings

Before anything comes out, we find out how far the water went. We take moisture readings so we know exactly what is wet and what is not, instead of guessing and tearing out more than we need to.

2

Remove Water Damaged Drywall & Insulation

We remove all of the water damaged drywall and insulation. Wet material has to go so the structure can dry properly and so the rebuild starts from clean, sound framing.

3

Structural Drying

With the wet material out, we dry the structure down with professional equipment. We keep drying until the readings say it is actually dry, not just dry to the touch.

4

Replace Insulation & Drywall

Now we put it back together. New insulation, new drywall, taped and finished, so the room is rebuilt to where it was before the water got in.

5

Paint

The last step is paint. We finish the walls so the room looks done, not patched. When we leave, you have a finished space, not a project someone else still has to close out.
