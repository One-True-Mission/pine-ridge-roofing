Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Pine Ridge Roofing and Mitigation collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep this simple on purpose.

What We Collect

When you fill out our estimate form, we collect the information you choose to give us: your name, phone number, email address if you provide one, your city or area, the type of work you need, and whatever details you write in the message. We do not ask for sensitive personal information, and you should not send any through the form.

Our website does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser type, for security and to keep the site running.

How We Use It

We use the information you send to respond to your request, prepare and discuss an estimate, schedule and perform work, and stay in touch about your project. That is it.

Contact by Phone, Text, and Email

When you submit the form and check the consent box, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text, or email about your request. Message and data rates may apply to text messages, message frequency varies, and you can reply STOP at any time to opt out of texts. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of hiring us, and you can always just call us instead.

We Do Not Sell Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We do not share it for anyone else's advertising.

Who Helps Us Process It

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run the site and handle your messages:

  • Formspree, which delivers the estimate form submissions to us by email.
  • Our website hosting provider, which serves the pages and keeps standard security logs.

These providers handle your information only to provide their service to us, under their own privacy and security terms.

How Long We Keep It

We keep the information you send for as long as we need it to respond to you and to keep a record of the work we discussed or performed. When we no longer need it, we remove it.

Your Texas Privacy Rights

If you are a Texas resident, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act gives you the right to ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we hold personal information about you and access it.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Delete information you have given us.
  • Opt out of any sale of your information, which we do not do anyway, or of targeted advertising, which we do not do.

To make any of these requests, call us at (903) 363-4384 or use the details on our contact page. We will respond within the time the law allows. If we cannot honor a request, we will explain why.

Children

This website is meant for adults arranging roofing and restoration work. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of the page. Please check back from time to time.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Call (903) 363-4384 or reach us through our contact page.
