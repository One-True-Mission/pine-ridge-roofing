Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Pine Ridge Roofing and Mitigation wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive technology.

Our Goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of abilities.

What We Have Done

This site was built with accessibility in mind. That includes descriptive alt text on images, clear heading structure, readable color contrast, keyboard-reachable navigation, and support for reduced-motion settings so animations calm down for anyone who prefers that.

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is not a one-time task. As we update the site, we work to keep it usable for everyone. We know there is always room to improve, and we take feedback seriously.

Need Help or Hit a Barrier?

If any part of this site gets in your way, or you need information from it in a different format, please tell us. Call (903) 363-4384 or reach us through our contact page, and we will help you get what you need and work to fix the problem.
